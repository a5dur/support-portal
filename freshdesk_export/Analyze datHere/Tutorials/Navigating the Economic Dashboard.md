# Navigating the Economic Dashboard

Navigating our Economics Dashboard is your gateway to unlocking a treasure trove of insights derived from Census data. With user-friendly filters allowing customization by year, summary, and name, exploring economic indicators has never been more intuitive. Delve into a rich array of charts and data representations, including median household income, employment status, per capita income, unemployment rates, poverty levels, and more, all sourced directly from Census data.

As you embark on your data exploration journey, harness the power of interactive filters to tailor your analysis to specific regions or demographic groups. Whether tracking changes in median household income over time, scrutinizing unemployment rates by geographical area, or assessing poverty dynamics across different demographics, our Economics Dashboard offers a seamless navigation experience. By leveraging Census data, users can confidently navigate through economic complexities, uncovering insights that inform strategic decision-making and drive positive socioeconomic outcomes.
